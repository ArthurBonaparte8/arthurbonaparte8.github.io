Claude Gmail Bridge

Claude Gmail Bridge is a personal, non-commercial tool built and used by Arthur Levy for his own needs. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Connects the owner's own Gmail accounts to Claude (Anthropic's AI assistant) through a small program that runs locally on the owner's computer.
  • Lets the owner ask Claude to search and read his emails, prepare draft replies, and send emails he has reviewed and approved.

Who can use it

Only the owner's own Google accounts. No other user is invited or supported.

Google permissions requested

  • gmail.readonly: search and read emails.
  • gmail.compose: create drafts and send emails the owner has approved.
