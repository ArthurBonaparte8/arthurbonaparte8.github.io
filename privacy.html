Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy describes how Claude Gmail Bridge (the "App") handles data. The App is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by Arthur Levy (the "Owner") exclusively for his own Google accounts.

Data accessed

With the Owner's explicit consent through Google's sign-in screen, the App accesses Gmail messages, threads and metadata (gmail.readonly) and Gmail drafts and sending (gmail.compose).

How data is used

  • Only to answer the Owner's own requests made to Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, such as finding, summarising or replying to emails.
  • Emails are only sent after the Owner has reviewed and approved them.
  • Data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

Storage and security

  • The App runs locally on the Owner's computer.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored locally in the operating system's secure credential store and are never shared.
  • The App operates no server or database and keeps no copy of email content beyond what is needed to answer a request.
  • Email content requested by the Owner is processed by Claude (Anthropic) to answer that request, under Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with any third party, other than Google (to access Gmail) and Anthropic (to process the Owner's own requests).

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting the App's local credentials.
